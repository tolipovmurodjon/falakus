# Submission rules and form (official, read 15 Nov 2026)

Sources: Space Apps Project Submission Guide, Judging & Awards Guide (13 Nov 2026), the guide video, and Murodjon's copy at `C:\Users\Murodjon\Downloads\NASA Space Apps Challenge.pdf`.

## Deadline
Click **Submit for Judging** before **Sun 15 Nov 23:59 Tashkent time** (local event). Only the last saved submission is judged, and nothing can be edited after the deadline. Use **Save progress** often; the lock toggle stops teammates overwriting each other.

## Rules that disqualify
- Demo over 7 slides or over 30 s: not eligible for global judging.
- Any link that needs login.
- Anything not in English (a video may use another language with English subtitles).
- Any picture, video or voice of a person under 18.
- Team members not all registered for the same local event and on the Members tab (max 6).
- SI images or video without a visible watermark; NASA logos in generated content.

## Judging: 1–5 on each criterion
Impact · Creativity · Validity · Relevance · **Presentation**. The guide says to give presentation equal weight.
Phases:
- Local judging → global nominees (3 Dec).
- Global finalists and honorable mentions (17 Dec).
- Executive judges → 10 global awards (26 Jan 2027).
Awards that fit us:
- Best Use of Science: walk-forward tests, null control, laser check.
- Best Use of Data: NISAR + ICESat-2 + Sentinel.
- Local Impact: 10.9 M people in the Fergana valley.
- Best Storytelling: plain-language briefings.

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Names and team |
| 0:02 | The challenge and why we chose it |
| 0:05 | Title and tagline |
| 0:10 | How it works: images, the user's experience, how NASA open data made it possible |
| 0:20–0:30 | The future: what it can do for people |

## Slide deck must show
Team name · member names · the challenge (Dancing with the SARs) · an image of the project · the user's experience · how NASA and partner data make it possible. Use readable fonts and few words.
