# 30-second demo video: storyboard (v2, the one story)

Screen recording of the live app at 1920×1080, English captions (large, bottom-centre), no voice needed.
Music: none or royalty-free. No NASA logos. Every number on screen is one the app prints itself.

| sec | screen (what is recorded) | caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | Cold open: chapter 1 opens over the Andijan reservoir, radar left, photo right | "The Fergana valley drinks from one reservoir." |
| 4-9 | Chapter 1 → "Cloudy day, 24 Sep": drag the white bar from radar (black lake) to the photo (white cloud) | "On 24 Sep the camera saw only cloud. NASA-ISRO's NISAR radar saw the lake." |
| 9-14 | "Play every pass": the lake shrinks pass by pass, frame info shows the km² falling | "Every 12 days, through cloud: 50.5 km² in July, 19.3 km² in October." |
| 14-18 | Chart on the panel: radar line next to the photo line | "Checked against every clear photo: r = 0.83." |
| 18-22 | Chapter 2 "Harvest map": drag across the red and green fields | "The water becomes crops. Radar sees 12.8 % of fields cut, field by field." |
| 22-26 | Chapter 3 Aral: drag 1997 radar → 2026 | "Where the river ends: 1,598 km² of sea in 1997, about 1 km² today." |
| 26-30 | Chapter 1 forecast slide, purple forecast line, then title card | "Falakus forecasts the next 3 months. Open source, any place on Earth." |

How to record: `/screen-record` skill on the laptop (ffmpeg, 1920×1080), one take per row, then cut and caption with ffmpeg.
Check before export: every caption number matches the app screen in the same shot.
