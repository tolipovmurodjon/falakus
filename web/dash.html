Why radar: the camera loses the lake when plans are made

In 2025, 29 of 85 Sentinel-2 visits (34 %) were mostly cloud; 15 of 24 in Dec–Mar, when release plans are made. NISAR's 24 cm L-band pulse goes through cloud, day or night.
