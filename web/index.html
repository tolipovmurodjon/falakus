How to read the radar (30 s)
  • white = towns and rough ground: walls + ground send the pulse straight back (HH)
  • grey = fields, plants, soil: part of the pulse returns
  • black = calm water or smooth sand: the pulse bounces away
  • in 20 m science areas: green = plants (HV, volume scatter), amber = very bright, blue = calm water
  • bright and dark sides of mountains = slope geometry, not change
