How to read the radar (30 s)
  • white = towns: walls + ground send the pulse straight back
  • pink = strong HH: rough or wet ground, rocks
  • green = more HV: plants tumble the pulse (also noise over very dark ground)
  • black = calm water or smooth sand: the pulse bounces away
  • bright and dark sides of mountains = slope geometry, not change

Colours are NASA's preview rendering of provisional NISAR data, not calibrated decibels.
