How to read the radar

Black: calm water. It acts as a mirror, so no echo returns. Green: plants. Leaves and branches scramble the wave (strong HV). Yellow: rough bare ground, rock, rubble, and towns (strong HH). Brightest: buildings. Walls and ground make a corner reflector.

Colours are NASA GIBS's false-colour rendering of NISAR L2 GCOV (HH and HV polarisations). This is backscatter (brightness), not ground motion: centimetre motion needs the interferometry product (GUNW).
